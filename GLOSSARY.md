# Events Glossary

## Member

A local representation of a person who has authenticated with Discord. A member is identified by their Discord ID, never by a display name.

## Verified Member

A member whose Discord account has been confirmed as belonging to The Last Ember Discord server.

## Administrator

A verified member with permission to manage every event. Administrators are assigned through a server-side operational process in this release; event creation does not require administrator access.

## Event

A planned community activity with a time, status, creator, and optional participation limit.

## RSVP

A verified member's active registration for one event. A member can withdraw an RSVP without explanation.

## Session

A time-limited server-side authenticated state for one verified member.
