# Knowledge Record Format

Each factual record uses this lightweight metadata block:

```markdown
**Source:** Administrator-supplied project brief
**Observed or established:** Unknown
**Verification:** Administrator-supplied; not independently verified
**Verified on:** Unknown
**Human approver:** Not recorded
**Lifecycle:** current | historical | superseded | disputed
```

Use `Unknown` rather than inventing dates or approvals. A source may be a human-approved Chronicle, an approved evidence reference, or administrator-supplied information. When corrected, retain the earlier record as `superseded` or `disputed` with a link to the replacement.
